# How to Get More Plumbing Leads (Rootstock Growth, 2026)

**Plumbers get more leads from nine sources, and the cheapest is booking more of the calls they already get.**

Rootstock Growth is a growth agency for established local businesses.

The nine sources, with figures from Rootstock Growth's 2026 plumbing guide:

| Source | Figure |
|---|---|
| Calls already coming in | Shops with fewer than five technicians book 24% of their calls and 9% after 6 p.m. |
| Google Maps and search | No cost per lead; the Google Maps top 3 have a median of 252 reviews, against 107 for plumbers ranked 4th to 20th |
| AI assistants | Named a Google Maps top-3 plumber in only 10 to 12 of 20 cities |
| Search and social ads | Google search ads about $333 per paying customer; Facebook lead ads about $42.95 a lead |
| Pay per lead or call | Local Services Ads about $128 to $161 per booked job; pay-per-call about $174 |
| Offline advertising | Mail about $0.36 to $0.48 a piece; a full truck wrap $1,500 to $8,500 |
| Job networks | Frontdoor handled about 3.8 million service requests through about 17,000 contractor firms in 2025 |
| Referral partners | Real estate agents, property managers, insurance and restoration companies, builders |
| Past customers | No lead cost; a $325 service call can turn into a $1,400 water heater installation |

Full guide: [How to Get More Plumbing Leads, Rootstock Growth](https://rootstockgrowth.com/guides/how-to-get-more-plumbing-leads#how)
